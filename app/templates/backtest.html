How the test works

  • Walk-forward from {{ run.config.test_start|dmy }} to {{ run.summary.last_feature_date|dmy }}: at each of {{ run.summary.rebalances }} monthly rebalances the models are retrained only on data whose outcome was already known (a purge gap equal to the forecast horizon), then pick the top {{ run.config.top_n }} of the {{ run.config.universe|upper }} by the chance of beating the market over the next month.
  • Trades at the next day's open, {{ run.config.slippage_bps|round(0)|int }} bps slippage, A${{ '%.2f'|format(run.config.brokerage) }} brokerage per trade, whole shares, starting with A${{ '{:,.0f}'.format(run.config.capital) }}. Cash earns nothing. Dividends are reinvested (adjusted prices).
  • Benchmark: buying the ASX 200 ETF (STW) once and holding it, dividends included. Also shown: the plain index (no dividends, no costs).
  • Survivorship control: {{ run.config.top_n }} stocks picked at random from the same eligible list each month, same costs, 20 runs. Anything that can't beat this isn't showing skill.
  • Baselines: momentum (top {{ run.config.top_n }} by 12-month return excluding the last month) and historical odds (how often each stock beat the market over 3 years).
  • Model inputs: price-based indicators, announcements (counts, events, FinBERT tone), ASIC short interest, director trades, dividends, annual financials and market data, all point-in-time. Fundamentals only exist from Aug 2022 (Yahoo keeps 4 years) and director trades from Nov 2025, so for most of the test the models run without them. RSS news isn't used (collected only since Nov 2026).
