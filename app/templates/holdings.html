How to enter corporate actions
  • DRP: the shares you received from a dividend reinvestment plan, at the DRP price (brokerage usually 0). The dividend itself is still counted as income.
  • Capital return: the shares it applied to and the cash per share. It lowers your cost base; any amount above the cost base counts as a capital gain.
  • Demerger: the parent's code, the new company's code, how many new shares you got per share held, and the % of cost base the company says to move across (from its tax statement). The new shares keep your original purchase dates for the CGT discount.
  • Takeover for cash: enter a Sell at the offer price. Scrip-for-scrip: enter a Sell and a Buy (this app doesn't apply roll-over relief). Rights issue / SPP: a Buy. Splits and consolidations are applied automatically.
